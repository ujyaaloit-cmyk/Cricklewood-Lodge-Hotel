London Travel

Important London places from the hotel.

Use the hotel as a North West London base for Wembley events, famous landmarks, museums, shopping, parks and onward travel.

Main Event Route

Wembley Park, Wembley Stadium and OVO Arena Wembley

Wembley is one of the most useful trips from the hotel for concerts, football and arena events. Public transport is usually the best option on event days.

Fast tube routeWalk to Kilburn Underground

Take the Jubilee line northbound to Wembley Park. The tube section is about 11 minutes from Kilburn, with a short walk at each end.

Simple bus/tube routeBus to Willesden Green, then Jubilee line

From Cricklewood, connect to Willesden Green and take the Jubilee line to Wembley Park. Rome2Rio lists this as about 15 minutes from Cricklewood Station to Wembley Park Station.

Cheapest direct bus optionBus 245 towards Wembley

Rome2Rio lists a direct bus option from Cricklewood Broadway/Claremont Road toward Wembley Park in around 28-29 minutes.

Taxi optionAround 3.6-4.1 miles

Rome2Rio lists taxi to Wembley Park Station at about 9 minutes from Cricklewood Station; hotel booking sites list Wembley Stadium about 20 minutes by car.

Famous Landmarks

Classic London sightseeing.

Big Ben & Houses of ParliamentWestminster

Take the Jubilee line from Kilburn toward Westminster. Good for first-time London photos and riverside walks.

London EyeWaterloo / Westminster

Stay on the Jubilee line to Waterloo, or get off at Westminster and walk across the bridge.

Westminster AbbeyWestminster

Use the Jubilee line to Westminster, then walk. Combine with Big Ben and St James's Park.

Buckingham PalaceGreen Park / Victoria

Use Kilburn Underground and change in central London, or travel to Green Park and walk through the park.

Tower of LondonTower Hill

Use the Jubilee line into central London and change for Circle/District line to Tower Hill.

Tower BridgeTower Hill / London Bridge

Pair this with Tower of London. London Bridge is also reachable by Jubilee line with onward walking.

St Paul's CathedralSt Paul's / Blackfriars

Use the Jubilee line and change in central London. Combine with Millennium Bridge and Tate Modern.

Trafalgar SquareCharing Cross

Travel into central London and walk from Westminster or change for Charing Cross.

The Shard & London BridgeLondon Bridge

Take the Jubilee line from Kilburn toward London Bridge for one of the simplest central routes.
